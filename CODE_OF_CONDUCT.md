# Code of conduct

kmra.dev is built for everyone, and its issues, pull requests and discussions should feel that
way too.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- Accept that people have different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience.
- Help keep threads on topic and useful.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or jokes
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission
- Spam, or repeatedly derailing a conversation

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who don't follow
this code. To report a problem, email **ping@kmra.dev**. Reports are kept confidential.
