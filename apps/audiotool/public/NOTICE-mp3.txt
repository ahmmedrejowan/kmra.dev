MP3 files are encoded in your browser with the LAME MP3 Encoder (https://lame.sourceforge.io/),
licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License (https://lame.sourceforge.io/license.txt).
Source: https://sourceforge.net/projects/lame/files/lame/

It reaches the page in one of two builds, both unmodified from npm:

- @mediabunny/mp3-encoder, a WebAssembly build of LAME for Mediabunny
  (https://github.com/Vanilagy/mediabunny). Mediabunny and the extension are licensed under the
  Mozilla Public License 2.0 (https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/).
- ffmpeg.wasm's libmp3lame, used when the browser's own encoders can't do the job. See
  /ffmpeg/NOTICE.txt for ffmpeg's licence (GPL).
